Carriers import (CSV)

Pehli line header ho: Company, MC, Equipment, Email, City, State, Phone, Officer (Company, Email aur City/State zaroori; baqi optional). Excel se Save as CSV karo ya seedha copy karke neeche paste karo. Header ke baghair bhi chalega: har line mein Company, Email aur City, ST ho (equipment na ho to wo har load ke liye dikhega).

Deadhead = seedhi doori x 1.2 (road ka andaza). Exact road miles button se top 10 carriers ke asli miles aa jate hain.

Placeholders: {first} {company} {equipment} {base} {pickup} {delivery} {deadhead} {miles} {weight} {rate} {date}. Jis line ka value khali ho wo line email se hat jati hai.
